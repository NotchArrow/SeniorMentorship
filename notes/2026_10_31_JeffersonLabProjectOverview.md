JLab's proposed project can be summarized as the following:
- Researchers propose experiments and projected resource usage, such as beam time in the particle accelerator.
- Beam time research is slow and costly, and can often require long wait times for projects to finish collecting data.
- When the particle accelerator beam is used, data is logged to indicate usage with precise metrics. However, this data is not always accurate, as usage for `calibration` cannot be easily attributed to a specific experiment or resource budget.
- This data is put on an existing dashboard, alongside data for publications following the research.

JLab wants the new dashboard to have accurate usage data by compiling ideas from multiple sources. The Department of Energy will use the new dashboard to monitor experiments and learn precisely where funding is going in the research.

Additionally, an AI model will be trained and used to evaluate research proposals, resource usage, publications, and preliminary data to decide when experiments are complete early, simply require more waiting time, or are not worth continuing. 

The new system is designed to provide context behind experiments that start and collect data, but do not publish for multiple years after that date.
