## Topic Information
Proposed research will work with the Department of Energy on research evaluation processes. 
An AI will be created to review the success of projects and prior proposals, and a new request dashboard will be developed to monitor research progress.

The AI development and use-case has not been finalized, but multiple avenues of exploration exist.
- AI could be used to develop early stopping techniques in research. 
> Early stopping can analyze preliminary results and experimental data for conclusions, and it can save time and resources if statistically significant results are identified. 
> An AI model would provide continuous monitoring and evaluation, without the risk of human p-hacking bias.

- AI could be used to supplement or replace human review in research processing, funding, and publication.
> Prior research argues about the effectiveness of AI in the research review process.
> Some articles conclude that models like GPT-4 are not suited for research tasks, since scientists found substantial differences between AI decisions and human reviewer evaluations.
> However, a specialized AI model may achieve more promising results in comparison with human reviewers.
